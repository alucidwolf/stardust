# Stardust

Read [`design-system/START-HERE.md`](design-system/START-HERE.md) before
changing anything visual. It is the approved design direction, and it names the
two documents that follow it and the tokens they are built from.

Everything here must be original. No Gundam or Cowboy Bebop names, designs,
emblems or quotes, and no combat: the instruments are for flying, not fighting.

`../currents` is the architecture reference. Read it freely; never modify it.
